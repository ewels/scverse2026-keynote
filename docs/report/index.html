Choose your own keynote What the audience did

Phil Ewels · scverse 2026 · DTU Lyngby · 12 October 2026

The keynote was a pixel-art game, and the audience steered it from their phones. 64 phones ranked the rooms, they sent around 630 messages, and asked 22 questions. 11 were answered on stage. The other 11 are at the bottom of this page.
