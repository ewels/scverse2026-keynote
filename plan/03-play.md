# Phase 7: play across the venue

Venue-wide systems and easter eggs. All work with the clicker alone (Left, Right, Action). Each egg calls `egg(id, label)` once so the Q&A screen can say "Easter eggs found: 7/20".

## Systems

- **Annoyance:** every NPC has a patience counter. Poking an NPC repeatedly gets replies that grow shorter and crosser (speech bubbles), then the fight egg.
- **Idle:** if the hero stands still for 25 seconds in any scene, nearby NPCs start talking to each other in speech bubbles, then one taps their watch.
- **Hero variants:** with Lukas or Luca as hero, hosts and the greeter react ("You're not Phil", "Organiser badge, then"). Nothing that flatters Phil: no fans, selfies or praise for the speaker.
- **Carry-over:** things done in one place show up later (the hat from the photo booth stays on, coffee cups pile up on the foyer table, a sticker from the hackathon appears on the hero's hoodie).

## Easter eggs

- **The cat:** only after the nf-core hackathon room has been visited (it is the cat from the cat photos beat), a cat appears now and then in the foyer and in places (walks in, sits, washes, leaves). Action near it strokes it: hearts float up and it purrs (a little "prrr" bubble). Stroke it three times and it follows the hero between scenes until a door closes on it. Cat photos in the hackathon room include this cat.
- **Too much coffee:** the coffee urn (foyer table and coffee queue) pours one cup per Action. Cups stack on the table. At 10 the stack wobbles; at 15 it falls and coffee floods the floor; the cleaner arrives with a mop and glares. The hero gets a jittery walk (faster, shaking) for the next place.
- **Start a fight:** poke any NPC six times. A cartoon dust cloud with stars and fists rolls around; two security staff come and march the hero out. The screen fades to the outside scene by the entrance with a toast "BADGE REVOKED". Walking back in, the greeter prints a new badge that says "VISITOR". The talk carries on where it was.
- **Moonwalk:** hold left while facing right (press left just after right) for two seconds and the hero moonwalks with a sparkle trail.
- **Clicker code:** Left, Right, Left, Right, Action within two seconds. Everyone in the scene does a little dance for three seconds.
- **Kick every plant:** the old plant kick stays in the foyer. Knock over every plant in the venue and the cleaner sighs and sweeps them all back.
- **Leave mid-talk:** walking back out past the entrance after the opener: it is now raining outside, and the next session's speaker is queuing at the door with a laptop.
- **The sea:** unchanged.
- **Wave at the window:** in the foyer, Action at a window: someone outside waves back. The fifth time it is a cow.
- **Badge printer streamer, projector "no signal", dinosaur, ticket mountain, and the place secrets** from `02-places.md`.
- **Phone reactions:** audience reactions from phones (phase 8) float up on stage. If reactions arrive from a third of the connected phones (at least three phones) within five seconds, the crowd in the current scene gives a standing ovation.
- **Hidden phone egg:** on the phone page, tap the scverse logo seven times to send a golden "★" reaction that makes the hero do a victory jump on stage.

## Limits

- Eggs never block the talk. Right still steps through beats in the middle of any egg; an egg in progress finishes or snaps back.
- Nothing flashes fast or full-screen bright (presentation on a big screen).
- The fight sends the hero outside but keeps every place's progress.
